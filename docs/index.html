Add the Impact Banner to your signature

Pick a version, copy it, paste it under your signature. About 30 seconds, and you only ever do it once — the numbers refresh themselves afterwards.

Black Recommended

Holds up in dark mode on phones, where a white banner shows as a bright slab.

White

Blends into a normal white email on desktop.

Then, in Gmail

  1. Click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  2. Scroll down the General tab to Signature.
  3. Click into your signature, put the cursor below your name and title, and press ⌘V (or Ctrl V).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

If the paste comes through as a broken image, your browser blocked the copy. Use the fallback below instead, or just drag the banner image straight from this page into the Gmail signature box.

Don't type the numbers anywhere else in your signature. They go stale the moment you do. The banner is the only place they should appear.
